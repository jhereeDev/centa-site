why centa

Most trackers record and analyse. Centa decides, then you spend, then it learns.

Payday is the unit of time

Offline. No account.

questions

Short answers.

Does Centa connect to my bank or GCash?

No. You log spending yourself, mostly from the widget. That is the point: one tap at the moment you spend, instead of a bank feed you check once a week.

What does Pro include, and is it a subscription?

Pro is a one-time purchase through the App Store or Google Play. It unlocks all widgets and sizes, unlimited envelopes and goals, encrypted backup and restore, and CSV export.

Which phones does it support?

iPhone with iOS 17 or later, and Android 8 or later. Home screen widgets on both; Lock Screen widgets on iPhone.

What happens if I lose my phone?

Your data is only on the phone unless you exported a backup. Export one from Settings, encrypted with a passphrase only you know, and keep it in your files or drive.

How do I delete my data?

Uninstall the app, or clear its data in your phone settings. There is no account, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side. More on deleting data.
